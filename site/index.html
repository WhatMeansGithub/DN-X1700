DN-X1700 Mac USB fix

Patch your Denon DN-X1700's firmware so its USB audio works on current macOS. Everything happens in your browser: your file is never uploaded, and this page contains no Denon firmware.

Read this first

Flashing firmware can permanently destroy your mixer. The mixer's updater erases the whole flash chip and rewrites it. If power is lost or anything goes wrong during the ~30 second write, the mixer will not start again and can only be recovered with an external flash programmer. This project is provided as is, without warranty, and the author accepts no responsibility for bricked mixers or any other damage.

Your settings and presets will be reset to factory defaults. Write down or photograph your settings first.

Tested on one unit with macOS 27 (Apple Silicon) and Windows. Full details: README.

4

Flash it

  1. Put X1700FW.BIN in the root of a FAT32 USB stick (MBR partition table; a small stick is safest). Keep the name exactly X1700FW.BIN.
  2. Mixer off. Insert the stick into the rear USB-A port.
  3. Hold MIC2 and DUCKING, switch the mixer on, keep holding until the display says "Version UP Mode".
  4. Press Master EFX INS 1. It shows "Version UP execute?" and "Ver.2000 >>> Ver.2000" (the version number does not change). To cancel safely, switch the mixer off here. Nothing has been written yet.
  5. Press Master EFX INS 2. Do not touch anything until "Version UP complete! Shut the power OFF" appears (about 30 seconds). Use stable mains power.
  6. Switch off and on. Re-enter your settings.

Then, for the Mac: Utility, System Setting: PC/MAC = MAC, USB mode USB B, choose the sampling frequency (44.1, 48 or 96 kHz), and power-cycle the mixer. Do this again every time you change PC/MAC or the sample rate. macOS follows the mixer's rate by itself.

If an error code appears on the display, write it down and don't switch off straight away; the updater retries by itself. To go back to the original firmware, flash Denon's original X1700FW.BIN the same way.
