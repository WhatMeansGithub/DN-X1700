Make your DN-X1700 work with modern Macs

Patch your mixer's firmware so USB audio and MIDI work with the built-in macOS driver: 8 in / 8 out, 24-bit, at 44.1, 48 or 96 kHz. It takes a few clicks, right here in your browser.

Nothing is uploaded No Denon code hosted here Free and open source

Have a DN-X1600? There is an experimental version for the DN-X1600 (not yet tested on a real unit).

Flashing firmware can permanently destroy your mixer

You do this at your own risk. This project is provided as is, without warranty, and its author accepts no responsibility for bricked mixers or any other damage.

  • The update erases the whole flash chip. If power is lost during the ~30 second write, the mixer won't start again and only an external flash programmer can recover it.
  • Your settings and presets will be reset to factory defaults. Write them down or photograph them first.
  • Tested on one unit, with macOS 27 on Apple Silicon and with Windows. Read the full README.

      Flash it with Denon's updater

      1. Copy X1700FW.BIN to the root of a FAT32 USB stick (MBR partition table; a small stick is safest). Keep the name exactly as it is.
      2. Mixer off. Plug the stick into the rear USB-A port.
      3. Hold MIC2 and DUCKING, switch the mixer on, and keep holding until the display says Version UP Mode.
      4. Press Master EFX INS 1. The display shows Version UP execute? and Ver.2000 >>> Ver.2000 (the version number doesn't change). To cancel safely, switch the mixer off here: nothing has been written yet.
      5. Press Master EFX INS 2. Don't touch anything until the display says Version UP complete! Shut the power OFF (about 30 seconds). Use stable mains power.
      6. Switch the mixer off and on again, then re-enter your settings.
      Then, set it up for the Mac In the Utility menu, System Setting: set PC/MAC to MAC, USB mode to USB B, and pick the sampling frequency (44.1, 48 or 96 kHz). Power-cycle the mixer, and do so again every time you change PC/MAC or the sample rate. macOS follows the mixer's rate by itself.
      If something goes wrong

      If an error code appears on the display, write it down and don't switch the mixer off straight away: the updater retries by itself. To go back to the original firmware, flash Denon's original X1700FW.BIN the same way. If the mixer shows nothing at power-up after an update, the flash chip needs reprogramming with an external programmer; see the README.
